NextUp Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

NextUp ("we", "our", "the app") is developed by Harpers Hive. This policy explains what information NextUp collects, how it's used, and your choices. Contact: harpershive@proton.me

Information we collect

  • Account information: email address and profile details when you sign up or sign in.
  • Watchlist and viewing activity: titles you add to your watchlist, mark as watched, or rate.
  • Taste profile: a preference profile ("Taste DNA") built from your watch history and ratings, used to personalize recommendations.
  • AI assistant conversations: messages you send to the in-app AI assistant, along with the assistant's replies, so it can hold context across a session.
  • Trakt account link (optional): if you choose to connect a Trakt account, we store an authorization token and import your watch history from Trakt to improve recommendations.
  • Basic usage data: app version, device type, and error/performance logs to keep the app reliable.

How we use this information

  • To provide core features: your watchlist, episode tracking, and release calendar.
  • To generate personalized recommendations from your taste profile and watch history.
  • To power the AI assistant's responses to your questions.
  • To maintain and improve the app's reliability and performance.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data for advertising, and NextUp does not display ads.

Third-party services

  • Supabase — hosts our database and handles authentication.
  • TMDB (The Movie Database) — supplies movie/TV metadata and artwork. No personal account data is sent to TMDB.
  • Trakt — if you link your account, used to import watch history and source curated lists.
  • OpenRouter / Google Gemini — process AI assistant conversations to generate responses. Messages sent to these providers may include relevant context from your taste profile and watch history to personalize answers.

Each of these providers processes data under their own privacy policies and only receives the minimum data needed to perform their function.

Data retention and deletion

Your account data is retained while your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data (watchlist, taste profile, AI conversation history, linked Trakt tokens) at any time by contacting us at the email below. We will delete your data within 30 days of a verified request.

Children's privacy

NextUp is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Security

Data is stored with Supabase (PostgreSQL) using encrypted connections in transit. Access to backend systems is restricted.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected with an updated "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to harpershive@proton.me.

NextUp Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

NextUp ("we", "our", "the app") is developed by Harpers Hive. This policy explains what information NextUp collects, how it's used, who it's shared with, and your choices. Contact: harpershive@proton.me

Information we collect

DataSourceWhy we collect it

How we use this information

  • To provide core features: your watchlist, episode tracking, and release calendar.
  • To generate personalized recommendations from your taste profile, watch history, and (if connected) Trakt data.
  • To power the AI assistant's responses to your questions about what to watch.
  • To operate, secure, and maintain the app's reliability.

We do not sell your personal information. NextUp does not display ads and does not use your data for ad targeting.

Analytics and crash reporting

NextUp does not currently use any third-party analytics or crash-reporting service (e.g. we do not use Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or Crashlytics). The only usage data we retain is the operational AI request logging described above, stored in our own database.

Third-party services we use

  • Supabase — hosts our database and handles account authentication. Supabase processes data on our behalf under its own privacy policy.
  • TMDB (The Movie Database) — supplies movie/TV metadata, artwork, and search results. We send title/content queries to TMDB; we do not send your account or personal data to TMDB.
  • Trakt — if you choose to link your Trakt account, we store an authorization token and import your watch history to improve recommendations. You can unlink this at any time in Settings.
  • OpenRouter — processes your AI assistant conversations to generate responses, routing requests to underlying model infrastructure providers. Messages sent may include relevant context from your taste profile and watch history so the assistant can personalize its answers.

Each provider only receives the minimum data needed to perform its function, and processes it under its own privacy policy.

Data retention and deletion

Your account data is retained while your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data (watchlist, taste profile, AI conversation history, linked Trakt tokens) at any time by contacting us at the email below. We will delete your data within 30 days of a verified request.

Children's privacy

NextUp is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Security

Data is stored with Supabase (PostgreSQL) using encrypted connections in transit. Access to backend systems is restricted to the app developer.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected with an updated "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to harpershive@proton.me.
